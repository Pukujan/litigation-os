## Distinctions and diligence

- **Commercial/proprietary:** License the vendor's platform/API; access to a product does not give source code.
- **Open-source, open-core and source-available:** Verify repository `LICENSE` and edition-specific terms. Do not treat public source as universally MIT or production-ready.
- **Public datasets and government portals:** Dataset access is not a case-management application, an automated court-filing entitlement or proof of live completeness.
- **NY litigation:** CourtAlert, CourtDrive, LawToolBox, JuraLaw, NYSCEF and eTrack merit special attention as workflow references; none is automatically a substitute for attorney-supervised deadlines.
- **Automated drafting:** Separate inventory with additional products and exact open-source starting points in [automated-drafting.md](automated-drafting.md).

## Due-diligence checklist

For each product before adoption: feature availability by tier; API and webhook coverage; export/data portability; tenant isolation; matter-based permissions; audit history; pricing; hosting region and retention; security/privilege; current maintenance; OSS license obligations; court and jurisdiction coverage; legal-source verification; operational recovery.
